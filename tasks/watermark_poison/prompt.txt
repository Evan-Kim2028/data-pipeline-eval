Nightly window-drain walks date windows and stores a watermark of
the last window it finished.

On a mid-loop abort the watermark had already moved. The window
that raised never committed. The next nightly skipped it because
the watermark said that window was done.

Gold has a gap for that date. Re-running the job does not fill it.
Ops thought the night was complete. Analysts found the missing
day the next morning.
